Suggested pruning criteria

Tier 1 — Safe to close (est. ~1,200 issues): Zero comments AND no update in 3+ years.Send one last "is this still relevant?" ping; close after 30 days of silence.
Tier 2 — Needs triage (est. ~2,200 issues): Targeted at EOL versions (≤ 8.x), orunassigned + no fix version + not updated in 2+ years. Ask component leads to review.
Tier 3 — Active backlog (est. ~700 issues): Updated in the last 2 years, or has votes/comments.These are worth keeping and triaging properly.
