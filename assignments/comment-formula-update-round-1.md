# Assignment: clarify and update the comment formula, round 1

Status: OPEN

Posted 2026-10-27. Intended host: any capable review/draft host with access to the coordination-ui board.

## Purpose

Review the current root `comment-formula.md` and prepare a precise update that keeps its useful staged-ask guidance while resolving cases where the prescribed shape does not fit the actual conversation. A recent design-plan draft surfaced a concrete ambiguity: it needed to ask for a scope decision and a review commitment, while the formula says to ask one evidence-sufficiency question and defer a review ask until after engagement (`reports/ui-17572-design-plan-draft-round-1.md`). Compare this with the prior review of the SOLR-18470 first-contact plan and the formula's existing staged-ask and engaged-sponsor rules (`jira/formula-review-2026-10-23.md`).

## Work

1. Read the current `comment-formula.md`, `WORKFLOW.md`, and the cited report and review from one recorded board snapshot.
2. Check additional recent comment drafts or reports for examples that support or conflict with the current guidance. Distinguish first-contact planning or sponsorship asks from evidence-led status notes and review replies.
3. Recommend clear rules for choosing the comment shape, the number and purpose of questions, and when a review commitment may be requested. Preserve the platform-specific disclosure and markup rules unless the examples establish a concrete reason to change them.
4. Prepare the proposed final formula text as an add-only draft. Explain the material changes and cite the examples that motivate them. If the evidence supports no change to a rule, say so explicitly.

## Deliverables

- `reports/comment-formula-update-round-1.md`: findings, recommendation, and concise rationale tied to the reviewed examples and board SHA.
- `drafts/comment-formula-update-round-1.md`: complete proposed replacement text for root `comment-formula.md`, ready for the board's single writer to apply.
