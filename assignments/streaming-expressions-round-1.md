## The audit

Per ticket: read the Jira ticket, the branch diff against its base, and the receipt. Verify the recorded state against the branch (a moved tip or a diff that differs from the record gets flagged, not silently adopted). For the gated tickets, judge PR readiness: premise, scope, changelog entry, and what the description must state or avoid claiming. For the three retire candidates and the held 11922, the audit's job is the call itself: state the evidence (the settling runs and premise runs are in the receipts), give a recommendation, and leave the decision to the owner; these are presented as owner decisions, not taken. Follow the standing rules: no new Jira ticket unless someone asks for one; a scope question goes in a draft's Limits or A Choice; a needed follow-up fix is named in Limits or a Choice with a stated plan to submit it; public PR text carries no internal process vocabulary (no gate, receipt, ledger, rc=0, "fresh JUnit XML", or "pre-fix proof" as a label); Proof sections state plainly what ran, at which head, what passed, and that the new test fails without the fix (where a receipt records the proof as partial, configuration-split, or inconclusive by construction, the draft says that instead). Titles must be accurate. Where a draft must name a Lucene version, name every version that applies: main and branch_10x pin Lucene 10.4.0, branch_9x pins Lucene 9.12.3; if one version is mentioned, the others that apply are mentioned too.
