| "text_general applies stopwords and (if you configure them) synonyms" | `text_general` (`managed-schema.xml:299`) has its query analyzer on by default: `stop` with `stopwords.txt` (line 311) and `synonymGraph` with `synonyms.txt`, `expand="true"` (line 312). ### 2b. Does a config-only change answer the ticket?

**Partly.** The ticket (Open; reporter Cassandra Targett, 2017-03-08; local packet `research/jira-context/SOLR-10252.json`) says the example's default spellcheck field and `text_general` are a poor starting point for users. It asks whether a better field type could be used.

- The packet's comment from James Dyer (2017-04-07) lists three options: (a) a comment explaining why `text_general` is not for production; (b) a second catch-all copyField using `text_ws`; (c) a dedicated spell field, with a comment that users must index into it.
- This branch takes option (a) only. A comment answers the documentation part of the ticket. It does not change the example's field or type, which is what the reporter's question was about.
- The premise also changed since 2017. The reporter describes the field as a copyField that gets its data from every other field in the index. In the current `_default` configset that copyField is disabled (see 2a).
