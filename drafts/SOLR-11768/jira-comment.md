This happens when docValues is turned on for a field in the schema without reindexing the documents that already exist. The Indexing Guide page on DocValues says a reindex is needed after changing field definitions to use docValues: the DocValues page of the Indexing Guide (solr-ref-guide, modules/indexing-guide/pages/docvalues.adoc) . Reindexing the affected documents, or reverting the field as you did, fixes it. A clearer error message for this case is tracked in SOLR-10305.
