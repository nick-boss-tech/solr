This happens when docValues is turned on for a field in the schema without reindexing the documents that already exist. The reference guide says a reindex is needed after changing field definitions to use docValues: https://solr.apache.org/guide/solr/latest/indexing-guide/docvalues.html . Reindexing the affected documents, or reverting the field as you did, fixes it. A clearer error message for this case is tracked in SOLR-10305.
