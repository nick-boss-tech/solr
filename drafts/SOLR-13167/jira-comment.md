The reference guide answers this question. In the nested documents section, Solr says it enforces id uniqueness for root documents within a shard but not for child documents, to avoid the cost of checking, and that clients must never give two documents the same id. Duplicate child documents, and the varying search results that follow from them, are therefore the client's responsibility to prevent. The page is in the indexing guide, under nested documents, at solr/solr-ref-guide/modules/indexing-guide/pages/indexing-nested-documents.adoc. Closing as answered by the documentation.
