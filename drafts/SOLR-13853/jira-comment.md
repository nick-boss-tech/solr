We reviewed this ticket against the current code. A shard's state is a stored value that collection operations such as split and restore set, and a replica going down or recovering does not change it. So a shard can show as active while its replicas are down, which is the behavior in your report. We have not yet started a cluster to confirm the state after a replica outage. We are leaving the ticket open for a decision on whether shard state should follow replica state. A useful next step is the Solr version and the CLUSTERSTATUS output from the time of the outage.
