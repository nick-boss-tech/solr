The check you hit now runs on the leader address for a follower node, even without sharding. In standalone mode, the leader's address must be listed in allowUrls in solr.xml, or the follower refuses to start the fetch with a 403. The old property (solr.disable.shardsWhitelist) no longer exists. The replication page does not say this yet, and we plan to add a short note there naming the current setting. Whether the check should relax for standalone followers is a separate question we are leaving for the maintainers.
