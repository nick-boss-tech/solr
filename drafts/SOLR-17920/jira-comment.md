We looked at this against the current main branch (498a09aea97a). When a Solr core is running, it keeps files open that it still reads, such as old index files after a merge or commit, and Solr's deletion policy decides when old files are released (solr/core/src/java/org/apache/solr/core/SolrDeletionPolicy.java). Space held by files that were deleted but are still open is released when the process stops, which fits what you saw. We have not confirmed that this is the cause for your nodes.

To check, the next time a node shows the gap, could you capture the open deleted files for the Solr process (lsof -p <solr pid> | grep deleted, or ls -l /proc/<solr pid>/fd | grep deleted), and tell us the Solr version and whether merges or large commits were running on that node? With that we can tell whether this is expected behavior or something to fix.
