Two separate loops in leader election can overflow the stack on main. Both are still in the code, and the first one is reproduced.

- **Failed watch.** `LeaderElector.checkIfIamLeader` calls itself again each time setting the watch on the previous election node fails ([line 161](https://github.com/apache/solr/blob/b73f03976fa17b857bc0a81bc8af2c6b1c31e0bb/solr/core/src/java/org/apache/solr/cloud/LeaderElector.java#L161)). There is no limit and no pause. This is the trace in the 2025 comments (`NoAuth`). A test where every watch attempt fails with `NoAuth` ends in a `StackOverflowError` after 9 seconds on [commit b73f03976f](https://github.com/apache/solr/commit/b73f03976fa17b857bc0a81bc8af2c6b1c31e0bb). A limit of three attempts, after which the exception is thrown, [fixes it](https://github.com/nick-boss-tech/solr/compare/b73f03976fa17b857bc0a81bc8af2c6b1c31e0bb...bee5c511da6917d9c62c436590b7969db02306e0). `LeaderElectionTest` passes with it: 6 tests.
- **Rejoin after a failed sync or leader publish.** `ShardLeaderElectionContext.rejoinLeaderElection` calls `joinElection` on the same thread ([line 494](https://github.com/apache/solr/blob/b73f03976fa17b857bc0a81bc8af2c6b1c31e0bb/solr/core/src/java/org/apache/solr/cloud/ShardLeaderElectionContext.java#L494)). A test that forces failed syncs shows the stack growing 12 frames over 4 rejoins. Each rejoin waits 5 seconds at the leader throttle ([line 127](https://github.com/apache/solr/blob/b73f03976fa17b857bc0a81bc8af2c6b1c31e0bb/solr/core/src/java/org/apache/solr/cloud/ShardLeaderElectionContext.java#L127)), so a real overflow takes a long run of failures and was not reproduced. [Running each rejoin on its own thread](https://github.com/nick-boss-tech/solr/compare/b73f03976fa17b857bc0a81bc8af2c6b1c31e0bb...bcea4f23c1944cf0383193c66fccdb9e6e624567) keeps the depth flat. The [test with its hook](https://github.com/nick-boss-tech/solr/compare/b73f03976fa17b857bc0a81bc8af2c6b1c31e0bb...64624f4726f49dc723e319f6c6aa52ba81f52189) is on a separate branch.
- [SOLR-11787](https://issues.apache.org/jira/browse/SOLR-11787) has the same call shape (`PeerSync`, `SyncStrategy`, `runLeaderProcess`) and looks like a duplicate of this ticket.

**Recommendation:** Keep open and fix both loops. The first is the smaller change and matches the three-retry limit mentioned in the December comment.
