## What happens today

**A quoted phrase with a slop feeds the slop number into the phrase boosts, as if it were a word.**

For `q="phrase query"~10 term` with `pf=text`, a document with `10 term` in its text field is boosted. A 2018 comment on the ticket shows the same `10 term` boost with `pf2=text`, as `(text:"10 term")`.

## What this change does

**The slop of a quoted phrase is skipped when the pf, pf2 and pf3 boosts are built. A boost after the slop, such as `~10^2`, is skipped too.**

- A clause of the form `~N` directly after a phrase is treated as the phrase's slop, such as `~10`. A boost after the slop, such as `~10^2`, counts too ([slop check](https://github.com/nick-boss-tech/solr/blob/e96a057439c879427bf07195fe6e18bed08a2c42/solr/core/src/java/org/apache/solr/search/ExtendedDismaxQParser.java#L296-L331)).
- The pf, pf2 and pf3 builder skips that clause ([skip](https://github.com/nick-boss-tech/solr/blob/e96a057439c879427bf07195fe6e18bed08a2c42/solr/core/src/java/org/apache/solr/search/ExtendedDismaxQParser.java#L344-L350)). The phrase in the main query keeps its slop.
- Behavior change, stated openly: words on either side of a phrase with a slop can now form a shingle. A phrase with no slop already works this way, because the phrase is left out of the words ([words](https://github.com/nick-boss-tech/solr/blob/e96a057439c879427bf07195fe6e18bed08a2c42/solr/core/src/java/org/apache/solr/search/ExtendedDismaxQParser.java#L348)).

## Proof

- `TestExtendedDismaxParser` 40 of 40 pass at `e96a057439c879427bf07195fe6e18bed08a2c42`, 0 failures. Verified 2026-10-07 at this head.
- On the base code with this test file, exactly one test fails: [`testPf2DoesNotUseSlopOfPhraseAsTerm`](https://github.com/nick-boss-tech/solr/blob/e96a057439c879427bf07195fe6e18bed08a2c42/solr/core/src/test/org/apache/solr/search/TestExtendedDismaxParser.java#L261-L308). The test fails without the fix. It checks the slop with and without a boost, the pf case, and that pf2 still builds shingles from ordinary words.

## Limits

**Some forms next to a phrase are not covered.**

- A decimal slop, such as `~2.5`, and a boost before the slop, such as `^2~10`, are not covered by this change. Covering them is a named follow-up: a pull request that extends the slop check to those forms, with tests.
- A bare word with a fuzzy or boost suffix next to a phrase, such as `foo~2 bar` or `foo^2 bar`, may reach the boosts the same way. We have not traced it. If a maintainer wants it checked, we will open a follow-up ticket and PR.
- A boost on a phrase with no slop, such as `"phrase query"^2 term`, is not covered. It may reach the boosts the same way. We have not run it. If a maintainer wants it checked, we will open a follow-up ticket and PR.
