## What happens today

**An atomic update rewrites a parent without its nested child documents when the schema has a catch-all ignored field.**

The schema defines a catch-all dynamic field (`*`) of type `ignored`, which is neither indexed nor stored. Nested child documents are stored under field names such as `child1` and `grandChild`, and those names match the catch-all. When an atomic update rebuilds the stored parent, the reconstruction skips every field that is neither stored nor doc-valued. The children go with those fields ([base code](https://github.com/nick-boss-tech/solr/blob/b5c71bc5573c4e31b4cee5a7965d73587fc0ae58/solr/core/src/java/org/apache/solr/handler/component/RealTimeGetComponent.java#L878-L880)).

## What this change does

**Reconstruction keeps nested child documents under a skipped field name, and still drops plain values.**

- For a field that is neither stored nor doc-valued, or that is a copy-field target, plain values are dropped as before. Child documents under that field name are kept ([RealTimeGetComponent.java](https://github.com/nick-boss-tech/solr/blob/d0f29b4630c03f14db715f5f23864694ec9e8327/solr/core/src/java/org/apache/solr/handler/component/RealTimeGetComponent.java#L873-L913)).
- This is wider than the test. A child-document field that is a copy-field target now keeps its children too. Before, the whole field was skipped.

## Proof

**`NestedAtomicUpdateIgnoredFieldTest` fails on the base code and passes with this change.**

- The new test indexes a parent with a child and a grandchild. It then adds 120 filler documents over 12 commits, which is meant to push the original add out of the update log. The atomic update must then rebuild the parent from the index ([NestedAtomicUpdateIgnoredFieldTest.java](https://github.com/nick-boss-tech/solr/blob/d0f29b4630c03f14db715f5f23864694ec9e8327/solr/core/src/test/org/apache/solr/update/processor/NestedAtomicUpdateIgnoredFieldTest.java#L58-L115)). On the base commit `b5c71bc5573`, the test fails with one failure.
- Verified 2026-10-07 at head `d0f29b4630c`: NestedAtomicUpdateIgnoredFieldTest 1 of 1; NestedAtomicUpdateTest 15 of 15; TestRealTimeGet 4 of 4. All pass.

## Limits

- A child-document field that is a copy-field target keeps its children on atomic update now. No test covers that case.
- The update log step depends on the limits in `solrconfig-tlog.xml`, which default to 100 records in 10 files. If those limits change, the original add may stay in the log, and the test would no longer reach the index path.
