**SOLR-18295: send ReplicationFactorTest updates to shard leaders first**

`ReplicationFactorTest` closes follower proxies on purpose. The inherited cloud-client factory sometimes routes updates to any replica, so a single add, a batch, or a delete can choose a follower that the test has just cut off. This change overrides `createCloudClient` in this test only, with leaders-first routing. The helper's other randomized settings are kept. Replication-factor assertions and proxy cuts and reopens are unchanged, and the explicit non-direct requests still reach the healthy leader and follower.

Proof, on this head: format, compile, Error Prone and module check pass. The whole class passed in 20 of 20 fresh runs with seeds 18295ABC0001 to 18295ABC0014. A synthetic baseline with the mute removed and any-replica routing forced failed in 4 of 20 runs. Those failures were transport errors before commit at a cut follower, not the lost-after-commit case, so this sample does not confirm the bug as described. The routing change is justified by the code path, not by a reproduced failure.

Limits: local runs on one aarch64 host, not CI. The baseline is a synthetic test branch, not untouched upstream. Twenty runs do not establish a failure rate. This is a fork-only alternative to #4563 (single-document retry, Jan Høydahl), which is still open; this change does not change that PR.
