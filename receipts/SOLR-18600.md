## What the patch does

- New `HttpSolrClientBase` (released fields, protected constructor, shared implementation moved from HttpSolrClient, concrete mime-type hooks) and new `HttpSolrClientBuilderBase` (the BuilderBase body at top level). `HttpSolrClient extends HttpSolrClientBase` and keeps `DEFAULT_URL_PARAM_NAMES`, the `builder(String)` factory, `toBuilder`, and a `builder()` that delegates to it. `HttpSolrClient.BuilderBase` extends the restored builder base and keeps only the abstract `build()`.
- Declared types restored per the design table: CloudSolrClient (field, abstract getHttpClient, both withHttpClient/withHttpClientBuilder overload pairs, createOrGetHttpClient, createOrGetLbClient, createHttpClusterStateProvider), CloudHttp2SolrClient, HttpClusterStateProvider bound, LBSolrClient.Builder bound and its `instanceof` check, LBAsyncSolrClient, ConcurrentUpdateBaseSolrClient (field, builder ctors, getClient), plus `SolrRequest.processWithBaseUrl(HttpSolrClientBase, String, String)` re-added with the HttpSolrClient overload delegating. In-repo callers adapted: SolrClientNodeStateProvider (return type), CloudSolrClientCacheTest (override signature).
- Changelog fragment `SOLR-18600-restore-http-client-base-classes.yml` and a Solr 10.2 upgrade-notes entry.
